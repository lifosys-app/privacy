Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 July 2026

Lifosys (“the app”) is operated by Sudhir Ranjan (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Lifosys does not have servers, and we never receive your data. Everything you record stays on your device unless you deliberately choose to back it up or export it.

01 — What stays on your device

Lifosys is a personal life-management app. Everything you enter is written to private storage on your own device:

  • Tasks, projects, areas, and notes you create
  • Habits and their completion history
  • Journal entries — free text, tags, moods, and any photos, videos, audio, or documents you attach
  • Financial records you enter yourself — transactions, amounts, categories, budgets
  • Health and wellbeing entries — weight, sleep, steps, energy, mood, and meals you log
  • Location, only when you choose to attach a place to a journal entry
  • App settings and preferences

This information is stored in the app’s private storage area, which other apps on your device cannot read. It is protected by your device’s own security, including any screen lock or biometric lock you have enabled. Lifosys also offers an optional in-app lock using your device’s biometric authentication.

We do not receive any of this information. There is no Lifosys server, no account system, no cloud database, and no automatic upload.

02 — What leaves your device

There are only four circumstances in which data leaves your device, and three of them require you to act.

Backup to your own Google Drive

If you choose to sign in with Google and use the backup feature, the app creates a backup file containing your data and uploads it to your own Google Drive account. The app requests only the drive.file permission, which limits its access to files it creates itself — it cannot see or touch any of your other Drive files. We have no access to your Drive or to the backup. You can delete it at any time.

Export to a file

You can export your data to a file and save it wherever you choose. The file goes only to the destination you select.

Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, the app reads your display name, email address, and profile photo, and stores them on your device so the app can greet you and show your picture. This information is not transmitted anywhere by us. Your profile photo is loaded from Google’s servers to display it. You are not required to sign in to use the app.

Location lookup

When you attach a place to a journal entry, the app asks your device for its location and may use Google’s services to convert that into a place name. The resulting location is saved with your journal entry on your device.

03 — Notifications

Lifosys uses Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send occasional announcements about new features and app updates. This registers an anonymous device identifier with Google’s notification service.

This channel is one-way. It allows us to send a message to your device. It does not allow us to read anything from your device, and none of your data is transmitted through it. You can turn off notifications at any time in your device settings.

The app also uses local reminders for tasks and habits. These are generated on your device and involve no network connection.

04 — What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics
  • We do not use tracking, advertising, or profiling technologies
  • We do not show ads
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone
  • We do not operate servers that store your data
  • We do not require an account to use the app

06 — Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device for as long as you keep the app installed. You can delete individual entries at any time from within the app.

Uninstalling Lifosys deletes all of its data from your device. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no deletion request to make.

If you have created backups in your Google Drive, those remain until you delete them yourself.

07 — Security

Your data is stored in the app’s private storage area, isolated from other apps by Android. You can enable an in-app biometric lock for additional protection.

Data transmitted during backup or restore is encrypted in transit using TLS.

For transparency: data stored on your device and within backup files is not separately encrypted by the app beyond the protections Android and your device lock provide. We recommend keeping a screen lock enabled on your device.

08 — Children

Lifosys is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children — and in fact, we do not collect information from anyone, as explained above.

09 — Your rights

Because your data never leaves your device except at your own direction, you have complete control over it at all times. You can view, edit, export, or delete anything in the app whenever you wish.

Under data protection laws including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the EU General Data Protection Regulation, and the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have rights to access, correct, and delete your personal data. In the case of Lifosys, you exercise these rights directly through the app, since we hold no copy of your information.

10 — Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
